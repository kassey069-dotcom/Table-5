About Table 5

       
       
           

More Than Just a Meal

           

                Table 5: The Chismis Edition is a restaurant concept created                 for people who love good food and great conversations.                 We believe that every meal can become a special memory when                 shared with the right people.            

           

                Whether you're catching up with friends, spending time with                 family, or simply enjoying your own company, there's always                 a place for you at Table 5.
